# 1. The Problem With Job Boards

Job boards optimize for volume, not fit. You open forty tabs, read forty descriptions, and by the third one you've forgotten what made the first one interesting. The real cost isn't time, it's judgment fatigue: the twentieth "exciting opportunity" gets the same five seconds of attention as the first. This chapter names the problem precisely before chapter 2 starts solving it: a job search isn't a browsing problem, it's a triage problem, and triage needs a rubric before it needs a tool.
