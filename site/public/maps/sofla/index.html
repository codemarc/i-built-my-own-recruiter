Layer 3: 10 financial advisory, crypto, and fintech companies, name tags on the left sidebar (opposite layers 1, 2 & 4). Blockchain.com relocated its U.S. headquarters to Miami; Cyclops (stablecoin/crypto payments infrastructure, Mastercard client, $20M Series A) is Miami-based with a second office in Vienna; Pantera Capital and MoonPay operate from South Florida but are headquartered elsewhere.
