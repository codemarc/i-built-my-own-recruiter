# Scoring Rubric (Template)

Sanitized 100-point rubric from [Chapter 4](../book/04-building-the-scoring-rubric.md). Adjust
the dimensions and weights to your own priorities — the weights must sum to 100.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | What a high score looks like |
|-----------|-------:|------------------------------|
| Compensation | 25 | Meets or beats target comp |
| Role shape (hands-on) | 20 | Builder role, not pure org management |
| Culture fit | 15 | Founder-led, merit-based, thin overhead |
| Domain / mission | 15 | Work you'd want to do regardless |
| Constraint tolerance | 15 | Accepts your dealbreakers cleanly |
| Trajectory / upside | 10 | Growth, equity, or optionality |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## Tiers

| Tier | Range | Action |
|------|-------|--------|
| Strong Yes | 85–100 | Outreach today |
| Yes | 70–84 | Outreach this week |
| Maybe | 50–69 | Watchlist |
| Do Not Pursue | 0–49 | Archive |

## Notes

- Calibrate on roles you feel neutral about, not the one you already want.
- A soft dealbreaker docks a dimension; a hard one is checked before scoring runs.
